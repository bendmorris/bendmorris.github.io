Streets of Style is a hybrid beat-em-up, incremental, and dressup game. You start out beating up thugs for money, collect enough to upgrade your outfit, and eventually are showered with money from those thugs when they recognize that your style exceeds theirs.

This game was made in 48 hours for Ludum Dare 41, with the theme "combine two incompatible genres." It used a new text engine I had written for HaxePunk that included dynamic text effects, like waves and rainbow colors, completely gratuitously. You can still play it online on itch.
